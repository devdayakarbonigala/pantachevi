Terms and privacy

This is a hackathon prototype. It may change or stop working without notice.

What this is

PantaChevi is a decision aid for chilli farmers. It is not a replacement for an agricultural expert, a pesticide label or your local agriculture office. Any spray or harvest threshold marked as a sample is a placeholder and has not been verified. Do not rely on this prototype for real farming decisions.

What we collect

The questions you type are sent to the server so that it can answer them. In this build the server does not store them. The demo field is fictional. No account, location or contact detail is collected.

Third parties

This prototype is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Amazon. Later builds may send question text to Amazon Web Services for processing, and this page will be updated before that happens.
